CHAPTER 05 · THE LITTLE THINGS

Not every memory
needs a big occasion.

Some favourite moments are just us, being us. No planning, no filter — fakt apan.

From college lunches in classrooms and gardens, to the tiffin I carried from my PG, to little Mumbai trips after my internship — our story has always been made of ordinary days that became special because we were together.

There are photos from different seasons of us: the early days, the time we found our way back, temple visits, evenings out, and our more recent adventures. Pan photos peksha mala tyanchya mage aslelya goshti jast athavtat — random bakbak, “khalla ka?”, ekmekanna lavkar zop mhanणं, mood off asla tari check karna, ani ek minute serious bolun pudhchya minute la bakchodi. 😂 I wish every photo could play back the sound of that day.

“Bhai, remember this?” — and mag ek photo baghayla suruvat karun apan 20 minutes tyachich story sangnar. 😂❤️
