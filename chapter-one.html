CHAPTER 01 · 9 OCTOBER 2024

One empty seat.
One very random beginning.

Cloud Computing lab. Seventh semester. Me, being late as usual. And you, sitting next to the only empty seat in the room.

We hadn't really spoken before that day. You were just another person from college, and I was just trying to get through the lab without getting caught unprepared.

Then came the assignment submission. You had done yours. I hadn't. And somehow, you casually offered me your laptop so I could show the professor your work.

I'll admit it: your assignment saved me that day. Full marks, too. 😂 Not exactly the most heroic first impression from my side, but hey — it worked.

And somewhere in that same lab, you noticed the manifestation wallpapers on my laptop. A tiny detail, but it's funny how the smallest things become part of a story you end up remembering for years.

Later, you messaged me on Instagram. The conversations started casually. Then placement season arrived. I was clearing online assessments, while you were struggling to get past some of them. I started helping you practise, sharing last-minute tips and figuring things out together.

It began with college, assignments and placements. No grand entry. No dramatic music. Just two people from small-town backgrounds, both a little gavthi in our own ways, slowly finding more reasons to talk.

We didn't know it was the beginning. We were just living an ordinary day that would become our favourite origin story.

Looking back, I still find it mad that the story started because I was late and there was one empty seat beside you. Life has a strange sense of humour, Niku.
