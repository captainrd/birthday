CHAPTER 06 · RADHE RADHE

Five days in Braj.
A little glimpse of forever.

Mathura, Vrindavan, Govardhan, Barsana, Bhandirvan, Gokul, Ramanreti — and you beside me through all of it.

That five-day Braj Yatra is going to stay with me for a long, long time. We were together almost 24/7, staying in a BnB and travelling across Braj on a scooty. We moved through Mathura, Vrindavan, Govardhan parikrama, Barsana parikrama, and then Bhandirvan, Gokul and Ramanreti.

In Vrindavan, being with the person I love in the land of love felt almost unreal. We were both absorbed in chanting “Radha Radha.” I often feel that Shri Radha Rani sent you into my life. I can't explain that feeling perfectly, but being there with you made it feel even more real.

People kept asking if we were siblings because apparently our faces have started looking similar. 😂 Others thought we were married. Some blessed us while we were taking blessings or giving donations, saying things like, “Yese hi khush rehna saath me… khup tarkki karo.” Every time someone wished us happiness together, I quietly wished that life would let us keep earning those blessings.

But Barsana was the moment that really got me. We did almost five kilometres of parikrama barefoot, and you stood with me on an empty stomach. That effort, that faith, and you being right there beside me — it felt like a peak moment. I don't think a photo can explain what that walk meant to me.

And then Bhandirvan. At the sacred place associated with Radha and Krishna's marriage, we did one parikrama holding hands. In my heart, it felt like we were taking our own saat padi, our own saat pheras. Not because anything official had happened, but because of what that moment meant to me. Just your hand in mine, in a place so full of devotion and meaning.

On the way back, I found it difficult to leave you and return home. Somewhere during those five days, you had started feeling like home to me. Not just the person I travel with or call when something happens — home, the place I don't want to leave behind.

Somewhere between “Radha Radha,” barefoot steps in Barsana, and holding your hand at Bhandirvan, I understood how deeply I want you beside me in the life ahead.

Radha Rani's blessings always, Niku. For us, for the road ahead, and for all the places we haven't seen together yet.
