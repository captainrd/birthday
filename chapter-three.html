CHAPTER 03 · NOVEMBER 2024 – JANUARY 2025

We said we would move on.
But we kept showing up.

This part wasn't easy. My mom didn't accept our relationship, and suddenly love had to sit beside fear, family, and decisions we didn't know how to make.

When I told my mom about you, she rejected the relationship. It hurt, and I didn't know how to handle it properly. From around 20 November to 7 February, there was distance between us. We were trying to accept that maybe we should move on, even though neither of us seemed to know how to do that completely.

We even went to Matheran — just the two of us — thinking of it as our first couple trip and maybe our last one. Imagine that: going somewhere together while telling ourselves we were supposed to be learning how to live without each other.

Then, on 28 November, I got placed. You had prayed to Shri Swami Samarth for my placement, and you had told me that once I got placed, we would go to Akkalkot together. I got the news, and that promise mattered. Even in a phase where we were trying to stay apart, we still had something we had said we'd do together.

So, around Datta Jayanti, we went to Akkalkot. We got close again. And yet, we still kept telling ourselves we should stay away.

That's the strange truth of that time, Niku: we had mutually decided to move on, but we were still together in all the ways that mattered. We were trying to create distance while continuing to find our way back to each other.

I don't want to pretend that everything was magically fixed in one trip. It wasn't. But your prayers, that promise, and the fact that we still showed up for each other became part of the story too.

Sometimes the heart takes longer than the decision. We said “let's move on,” but our feet kept taking us towards each other.
