CHAPTER 04 · 7 FEBRUARY 2025

“I want to meet you once.”
And there you were. Again.

I was home until 7 February, because my internship was starting on the 8th. We'd had no contact during that stretch. Then you called and asked to meet me once.

When I came back to Mumbai, you said you wanted to meet me once. I said okay. We met, and I took you to my favourite plane-spotting place in Sakinaka.

And then, while we were travelling in an auto, you gave me a rose and confessed your feelings again.

After all that distance. After all those conversations about moving on. After trying to convince ourselves that we had made the sensible decision. You still came with a rose and told me how you felt.

And me?

Vhaa, me firse pigal gya. 😭🌹

What can I say? I can make plans, I can overthink, I can try to act like I've got everything figured out. But when it comes to you, one rose was enough to undo all that determination.

From there, we started finding our rhythm again. You were mostly at the PG during the day while I went to the office for my internship. After office, we'd meet and take little trips around Mumbai — nothing huge, just small pockets of time that belonged to us.

And by May–June, we made it to Lonavala for two days, staying in a private villa. Another first, another memory, another little chapter that was ours.

Since 7 February, we chose each other again. We haven't looked back in quite the same way since. We still had things to learn, and we weren't suddenly perfect. But that rose became a reminder that sometimes love returns not with a grand plan, but with one honest gesture.
