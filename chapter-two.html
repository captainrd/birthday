CHAPTER 02 · 18–19 OCTOBER 2024

The night got longer.
And then there was Aksa.

One almost sleepless conversation, a plan made at 6 in the morning, and my favourite place becoming the place where everything changed.

On the night of 18 October, we talked almost the entire night. One message became another, and suddenly it was around 6 AM. Somewhere in that conversation, we decided we would meet that evening at 6.

I took you to Aksa Beach — my favourite place, the place where I used to sit alone for hours when I needed to clear my head. I never imagined that I'd bring you there and leave with a memory that would stay with me like this.

You came dressed traditionally, and honestly, I was already gone. I was completely smitten. But when it came to actually saying yes to a relationship, I got scared and started overthinking. I told you we should stay friends and see what happened later.

You wanted an answer abhi. I still said no.

And then, on the way back towards the metro, in that auto, you started crying. I saw what my hesitation was doing to you. Somewhere between my overthinking and your tears, I realised I didn't want to let you go just because I was afraid of what came next.

So, yes. I said yes. ❤️

19 October 2024. That's the day we became us. From that day, I'd bring my tiffin from the PG and we'd have lunch together. Very simple things, but they became ours.

You gave me your favourite Ganpati Bappa — your Ganu. I still keep him in my devghar. I don't think you could have known then how meaningful that little gift would become to me. In a way, you gave me something that still sits right beside my faith, in the place where I pray.

Our relationship didn't begin with a perfect speech. It began with a beach, an auto ride, tears, one yes — and your Ganu finding a place in my home.
